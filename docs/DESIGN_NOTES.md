# Design notes: tried and rejected

Running log (§7.8). Newest first.

- *2026-07-30 · the hatched epilogue, the faintest lens, and the machinery of lives (v2/M18).* Six surfaces, one question under all of them: how does a page say what kind of statement it is making?
  - **The epilogue's register.** A speculative era has to be visibly a different kind of thing from a derived one, and it has to stay that way three screens in, because a reader who scrolls into it otherwise carries the same trust forward. `EraHeader` opens it with a notice-register stamp ("not history: a projection past the horizon") followed by a hatched bar that runs out to the end of the line: `.speculative-hatch` (`apps/web/src/styles/index.css`) is a 135-degree repeating gradient in the rule color at half opacity, a hairline every six pixels, which reads as the draughtsman's convention for unsurveyed ground rather than as an alarm. Amber and not thread red: the epilogue is the app talking about its own output, which is precisely what the notice register was minted for in M13, while red would file the projection as a divergence. Greying the era out would have said the wrong thing anyway, on the same grounds that cost the old grey "mock" chip its job in M13: unreadable is not the same as unattested.
  - **The philology tick.** `--color-lens-philology` is the faintest tick of the family in both themes (`#6a5a52` on Survey, `#b0a096` on Nitrate). The lens collects the events that moved a name, and most eras move none; a tick as loud as the political one would lie about how often it fires. The 3px earth-tone tick-mark rule from the original five holds unchanged, this one simply sits quieter inside it.
  - **The convergence card** on the event detail page is blue throughout: a record-blue left border, record wash, the "◉ convergence with the record" stamp, and the explanation in mono at the right ("institutional attractor · 40 years late", or "on schedule", which is its own kind of finding). The similarity note reads in ink below, the path note under that in faded ink, because how the road differed is a gloss on the claim rather than the claim. The reservation rule licenses the blue even though the card sits on a divergent event: a convergence is the attested record reasserting itself, which is the one thing on a derived page that record blue is actually for. Thread red would have said the opposite.
  - **The name-drift gloss** takes no color at all: a plain rule border, a faded stamp ("the record calls it otherwise"), and a definition list whose term is the drifted name with its kind and attested form in parentheses, and whose definition is the one line on how it happened. Neither reserved color fits. Blue would claim the drifted name is attested, red would promote every renaming into a divergence of its own, and a name is a gloss on the event above it either way.
  - **The lives block** sits under the dossier's description, in the mono metadata voice: born (or founded, by entity type) with the ending year folded into the same line when this branch has ended it, then the counterfactual mark, then "follows" with the predecessor as a link. The line appears only when the record fixes a beginning; a null birth year prints nothing rather than a placeholder. The counterfactual mark is a notice-register stamp reading "no counterpart in the record", the explanation on hover: it is the app telling the reader something about its own output, exactly like the DEMO pill, and leaving invented people indistinguishable from attested ones is the kind of quiet dishonesty M13 set out to end. **Tenures** get their own section headed "offices held, as this branch records them", role against span, one row each. The qualifier in that heading is not ceremony: tenures are replay-derived, so a sibling branch genuinely does record them differently. An office still held reads "onward" instead of a dash, on the same principle as the prev/next rail's terminal phrases: a dead end should say where you are.
  - **The epilogue toggle** joins the derivation fieldset beside the court, as another plain `aria-pressed` control with what it does in the hover gloss, and the standing prose line under the fieldset gains a clause while it is on. Nothing about a projection is worth a modal.
- *2026-07-30 · the contested mark, the court transcript, and the axes (v2/M17).* Three surfaces, one question each about what a color is allowed to mean.
  - **The contested mark** is deliberately colourless: `[contested]`, italic, in faded ink, with the chairs' unsettled reading carried as the title attribute. Record blue and thread red are both wrong here (it is neither attested history nor a divergence), and the disputed stamp's weight is wrong too: a dispute is a verdict, a contest is an archivist noting in the margin that two readings survived. It sits beside the disputed and convergence marks on the card and in the detail header, and an event can honestly carry all three.
  - **The court transcript** renders on the event detail page as a bordered section on raised paper: a quiet stamp reading "the court of plausibility", the outcome in mono at the right, then a definition list whose mono terms ("for the event" / "against" / "the ruling" / "as instructed") sit over prose. Rejected: speaker bubbles or any chat-log framing. The court is a record, and a record has a form; two named briefs and a ruling belong in the same typography as a ledger entry, not a transcript of a conversation that never happened.
  - **The axes flyout** hangs off the master determinism dial as a quiet mono disclosure ("axes", which becomes "axes (set by hand)" once any axis is off the master). Inside, three shorter sliders and a switch on raised paper. Their thumbs are faded ink, not thread red: the master dial keeps the red because the dial IS the divergence control, and its children are settings. The outputs read as words rather than numbers ("structures act" / "balanced" / "individuals act"), since the number was never the point. A "hand them back to the dial" reset appears only when there is something to hand back, and the standing line above says the rule plainly: each axis follows the determinism dial until you move it, then it is yours.
  - **The derivation controls** are plain `aria-pressed` toggles in the composer (standard / symposium, and the court beside them), with the cost in the hover gloss and a standing prose line under the fieldset restating what the current choice means. Rejected a switch or a modal for symposium mode: it costs roughly four times the tokens, so it needs a label a reader can weigh, not a toggle they flick.
- *2026-07-30 · the record room and the catalogue's hints (v2/M16).*
  - **The record room** (`/record`, reached from a Settings section rather than the shell nav: it is a curation tool, not a reading surface) is blue throughout, and unusually so: everything on it is attested history, so the reservation rule licenses a blue heading and a blue mono year in a right-aligned fixed column, with title, summary, and a mono metadata line (regions, themes, magnitude, pull) beside it. No thread red appears anywhere on the page, because no divergence does. Filters are native `select`s and a plain `type=search` input in the same mono border style as the ledger toolbar; no custom comboboxes, because 1578 rows do not need a search UI, they need a filter row. The flag affordance is mono and faded, going amber (the notice register) on hover, and it copies a structured report line to the clipboard, then reads "copied" for a beat. Rejected: editing in the browser. There is deliberately no CRUD here; the record is edited in the repository, with review, and a flag is how you say so.
  - **The catalogue composes, it no longer creates.** Clicking a gallery divergence used to derive it immediately. It now fills the composer and prefills the interpretation card from curated hints, then scrolls back to the top: the same confirmation step every typed divergence gets. The card drops what it cannot honestly show (no candidate chips, no clarifying question) and replaces the confidence figure with "from the catalogue", because nothing read the ask and there is nothing to be confident about.
- *2026-07-29 · the engine room (v2/M15).* An inspector, not a spectacle: mono-first typography, a run-grouped call list on the left (template id + version, model, tokens, cost, retries as a quiet stamp; failures in thread red because a failed call IS a break in the causal machinery), and flat sheet panes for system/prompt/response with copy buttons. Rejected: syntax highlighting and collapsible JSON trees (this is a reading room for prompts, not a devtools clone), and auto-refresh polling (a derivation's calls appear on next visit; the meter already narrates live).
- *2026-07-29 · the interpretation card (v2/M14).* Between typing and deriving now sits a reading: a plain sheet inside the composer carrying a thread-red "the reading" stamp (this IS the divergence taking shape, so the red is earned), a mono confidence figure, candidate mechanisms as bordered chips (selected chip takes the thread border; chips carry their rationale as title text), editable statement/year/region/mechanism fields on the raised paper, and the baselineContext under a record-blue "the record:" label since it is attested history. Rejected: a modal (the card is part of composing, not an interruption) and auto-deriving from the top candidate without showing the reading (the whole point of v2 is that the engine answers the question asked, visibly). The power-user escape is an explicit quiet "Just derive" button, not a hidden default. A clarifying question, when the reading is unsure, renders in the amber notice register: uncertainty is a mode warning, not a divergence.
- *2026-07-29 · the notice register (v2/M13).* Demo-mode honesty needed a third color family: record blue and thread red are reserved absolutely, and the old grey "mock" chip whispered when it had to speak. New tokens `--color-notice` / `--color-notice-wash` (parchment amber: `#7c5310` on Survey, `#d9a45b` on Nitrate, both AA on their papers) carry mode banners and, later, budget warnings. First uses: the shell's DEMO pill (uppercase stamp, wash-filled, links to Settings) and the composer banner stating plainly that demo content is canned and the prompt shapes only the frame. Rejected: thread red for the banner (divergence-only), and a dismissable toast (honesty should not be dismissable).
- *2026-07-24 · the seal.* A circular brand mark (the split hourglass between a blue and a red column, 1876 in the ring) joins the system: every white pixel un-matted to true transparency (white treated as the matte and un-blended, so the art recomposites exactly over white and prints as pure ink everywhere else), placed above the Atlas wordmark and beside the README title. The square hourglass mark becomes the favicon set (32/64/180) with its navy field flooded out, so the tab icon floats. On Survey the seal reads as ink, on Nitrate as an engraving lifted by a slight brightness filter. Earlier revisions kept enclosed whites (a wax-seal look) and the favicon's navy plate; both were rejected for full transparency. Record blue and thread red in the mark carry their reserved meanings (the record column, the divergence column), so the reservation rule holds.
- *2026-07-23 · the 0.2 interaction pass.*
  - **Burn confirmation** replaced `window.confirm` with a React Aria alertdialog restating what burns (title, counts) and offering "export first" inside the dialog: the escape hatch lives where the danger is. Undo/soft-delete was considered and deferred: a wrong burn is recoverable via export-first or the demo JSON, and a tombstone table is real schema weight.
  - **Compare checkbox recolored** from thread red to neutral ink: selection is UI state, not divergence, and the red reservation is absolute. (The one violation found in the audit.)
  - **j/k unified with DOM focus.** The visual ring previously tracked a separate index from real focus: two focus models, screen readers heard nothing. Now the walk moves actual focus to the card's title link; `focus-within` renders the same ring. Rejected a roving-tabindex rebuild as overkill for a list whose cards are single links.
  - **Stop control** sits beside the "deriving…" stamp in the same quiet border style as `+100y`: interruption is a normal act, not an alarm; thread red stays on the act of derivation itself.
  - **Search** is a plain `type=search` input at the toolbar's lens end, mono placeholder, `/` to focus. Rejected a modal command-palette: the ledger is one list; filtering it in place keeps the spine visible.
  - **Event cards** gained the stretched-link pattern (whole card clicks, inner entity links raised): the hover affordance finally matches the hit area. Intra-card focus moves no longer flicker the thread overlay (boundary-crossing check).
  - **Prev/next in event detail** as a quiet mono rail above the title, with terminal phrases ("the divergence is the beginning" / "the horizon, for now") instead of disabled arrows: dead ends should say where you are.
- *2026-07-22 · screenshot review, all views (mock, Survey + Nitrate).* Atlas / Timeline / Delta / Detail / Compare / Artifact reader captured and reviewed against §7.6:
  - **Timeline** reads as the thesis: blue record rail splitting at the POD header, red thread rail peeling off, record ticks in blue mono interleaved by year, rubricated Fell drop caps. Kept.
  - **Record ticks** were first drafted right-aligned against the blue rail with negative margins; the alignment fought the virtualizer. Rejected in favor of ticks flowing in the content column with the date column shared with events: alignment by sort order, not pixel math.
  - **Delta**: first tried d3 tidy-tree (top-down). Rejected: a downward tree reads as an org chart, not a history. Replaced with year-scaled vertical lines: blue trunk, red child threads leaving at fork years, plus an accessible list fallback. The child "compare" checkbox label can kiss the curve at tight fork angles. Acceptable; revisit if trees get dense.
  - **Compare**: shared-prefix rows washed in `record-wash` with a small `shared` stamp beat an earlier idea of collapsing shared rows entirely (history you can't see isn't history).
  - **Mock content nit** caught on the compare shot: the critic-repair replacement left a lowercase sentence opener. Fixed at the source (mock repair capitalizes).
  - **Fell for the "against"** in compare headers: one word of ceremony, 28px, within the ≥24px rule.
